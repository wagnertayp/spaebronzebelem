DAP (Drenagem de Alta Performance) is served by order of arrival, not by appointment. It covers the abdomen, legs, and arms; takes about two hours; includes approximately 40 minutes under a blanket, 40 minutes of drainage, and nutraceuticals; and costs R$ 200. When a client asks about DAP, explain these details and send both attached DAP images. Do not promise outcomes, weight loss, or toxin elimination.

**Why:** the user supplied the service details and asked to explain the walk-in process and share both images when clients show interest.

**How to apply:** Treat DAP as a walk-in exception only; do not infer that other services are walk-in.
