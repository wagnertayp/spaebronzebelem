---
name: WhatsApp booking assignment
description: Product decision for how client-selected WhatsApp bookings enter the staff agenda.
---

When a client completes a WhatsApp booking, keep the selected time provisionally reserved and create an unassigned appointment in the agenda queue. The client should be told that the reservation is provisional and that the team will confirm who will perform the service. Staff manually assign the attendee; validate availability again, then confirm the appointment and send the normal confirmation.

**Why:** The user chose this flow so the team can manually select who will attend after a WhatsApp booking, while ensuring the appointment appears in the panel and its time remains held.

**How to apply:** Keep provisional bookings visible in the agenda independently of the selected day, prevent conflicting assignments, and do not treat them as confirmed appointments or create work orders until a staff member assigns the attendee.
