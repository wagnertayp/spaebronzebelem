name: Pregnancy and tanning policy
description: Spa policy for tanning requests during pregnancy.
---

The spa does not provide any type of tanning to pregnant clients, as a precaution. When a client confirms she intends to visit for tanning and has not already shared her status, ask discreetly whether she is pregnant or could be. If she is, may be, or is unsure, decline tanning; do not send the visit bundle. Only after she confirms she is not pregnant, send the towel-and-bikini reminder, Neon banner, and configured address.

**Why:** the user stated this is the spa's policy and asked that the pregnancy check happen discreetly before confirming tanning visit details.

**How to apply:** ask only in the tanning visit flow, phrase the question gently, and decline tanning without making medical claims. Do not extend the restriction to unrelated services.
