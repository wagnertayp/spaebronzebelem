Describe those concrete deliverables without promising results or pitching during an arrival update or after the client has already chosen.

**Why:** The user reported that arrival messages were incorrectly met with “Qual serviço quer agendar?” and asked the agent to make the Neon protocol its priority recommendation, focusing on what it delivers.

**How to apply:** Handle clear arrival-status phrases before the AI booking flow. Keep the Neon recommendation in the bronze-selection context, use the catalog's three-session-plus-exfoliation offer and price, and do not interrupt a client who is already on the way or has booked.
